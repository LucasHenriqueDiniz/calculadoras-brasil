# Connect your site to AdSense — Google AdSense Help

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · The site-connection flow, read to settle whether a review looks at the whole site or only at what changed.

## What it says

- After the site connection is verified, the last step is to click **Request review**.
- The review covers the whole site: "We review your entire site to check it complies".
- It "usually takes a few days, but in some cases it can take 2-4 weeks".

## What it does not say

- Nothing about a cooldown, or about the button going inactive after rejections.
- Nothing about whether a second review weighs the changes since the first.
