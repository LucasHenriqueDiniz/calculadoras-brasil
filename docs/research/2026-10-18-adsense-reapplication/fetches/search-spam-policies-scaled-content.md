# Spam policies for Google web search — Google Search Central

> Fetched 2026-10-18 (page last updated 2026-08-28) · Read for the "scaled content abuse" definition, because 52 of the 53 public route files under `src/routes/` were first committed between 2026-06-23 and 2026-06-26, 40 of them on 25–26 June (`git log --follow --diff-filter=A`).

## What it says

- Scaled content abuse is "when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings". The page adds that this usually means a lot of unoriginal content with little or no value to users, however it was created.
- Examples include using generative AI or similar tools to produce many pages without adding value for users, and stitching together content from other pages without adding value.

## What it does not say

- This is a **Search** policy, not an AdSense one. No AdSense page fetched says the AdSense review applies it.
- It sets no page count or publishing rate. Publishing fast is not itself the violation. Many pages without added value is.
