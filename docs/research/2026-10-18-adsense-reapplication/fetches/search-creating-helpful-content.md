# Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content — Google Search Central

> Fetched 2026-10-18 (page last updated 2026-10-05) · Read for two of the pitch's questions: what counts as added value, and whether authorship matters.

## What it says

- Its self-assessment asks whether content offers "original information, reporting, research, or analysis", goes beyond the obvious, and provides substantial value compared with other pages in search results.
- Content drawn from other sources should add substantial originality, not lightly rewrite them.
- **Who:** it should be clear who created the content, with bylines where readers expect them. Fabricated author profiles, such as AI-generated headshots, invented names or false credentials, are deceptive.
- **How:** the page should explain how the content was produced. Where readers would reasonably wonder, it should disclose automation or AI use and why that was useful.
- **Why:** the page calls this perhaps the most important question. Content should exist mainly to help people. Automation used mainly to manipulate rankings breaks the spam policies.

## What it does not say

- This is **Search** guidance. No AdSense page fetched cites it. The link to the AdSense review is an inference: both ask for original value, and the AdSense rejection wording (`adsense-account-not-approved.md`) uses the same vocabulary.
- It does not require a byline. It says "where one might be expected".
