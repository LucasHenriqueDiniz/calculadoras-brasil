# Inactive sites and requesting a review — Google AdSense Help

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · Search snippets attributed the "button inactive after several rejections" sentence to this page. Fetched to check it.

## What it says

- A site that has shown no ads for at least four months is considered inactive, and has one month to start showing ads again.
- Before requesting a review, the AdSense code should be on the site and the site should comply with the Program policies.
- The review checks that the site is ready to show ads.

## What it does not say

- **The rejection-limit sentence is not on the live page.** The same was true of https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/12171038?hl=en, fetched the same day for the same reason, and its content matched this one. The sentence exists only on the Policy center page, `adsense-fix-policy-issues.md`.
