# Check the status of your AdSense sites — Google AdSense Help

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · Google's definition of the three site states the Sites page shows, read to learn what "Requer atenção" means and how a review is requested from it.

## What it says

- **Needs attention** means issues must be fixed before the site is ready to show ads. The page sends the reader to a separate article on what to do when a new site is not ready.
- **Getting ready** means Google is checking the site. A review "usually takes a few days, but in some cases can take 2-4 weeks".
- **Ready** means the site can show ads, as long as it keeps following the Program policies.
- A review starts from the **Request review** button. If the account setup tasks are not complete, the site is not reviewed and stays in Getting ready.
- Removing the site and resubmitting it can delay the process.

## What it does not say

- It names no rejection reason. "Low value content" does not appear on the page.
- It sets no limit on how often a review can be requested.
