# Google-served ads on screens without publisher-content — Publisher Policies Help

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · The Publisher Policies text closest to "low-value content", part of the "Inventory value" group.

## What it says

- Google-served ads are not allowed on screens "without publisher-content or with low-value content", on screens under construction, or on screens used for alerts, navigation or other behavioural purposes.
- Google may limit or disable ad serving on low-value or unfinished pages until changes are made.
- The examples include dead-end screens (thank-you, exit, error pages) and automatically generated content with no manual review or curation.

## What it does not say

- It does not define what makes content "low-value". The only operational examples are no-content screens and uncurated auto-generated content.
- It does not say whether navigation or hub screens count as "used for navigation". That reading would cover `/calculadoras` and `/comparar`, but it is an inference.
