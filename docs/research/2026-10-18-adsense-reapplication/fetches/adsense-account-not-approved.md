# Your AdSense account wasn't approved — Google AdSense Help

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · Google's own list of rejection categories, read for the closest primary wording to "Conteúdo de baixo valor".

## What it says

- The content-quality category says Google believes the site lacks enough original, rich content of value to users.
- Pages need "sufficient text". Sites made mostly of images, video or Flash may not be approved, and content should be complete sentences and paragraphs, not only headlines.
- Ad code should not sit on auto-generated pages or on pages with little to no original content.
- The site should be fully built and launched before applying, with the ad code on a live page.
- A linked video is titled "Thin content (and why quality content matters)".

## What it does not say

- No word count, page count, site age or traffic threshold.
- The literal phrase "low value content" does not appear. The closest wording is "original, rich content" and "of value to users".
