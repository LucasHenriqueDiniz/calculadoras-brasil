# Saiba quem deve declarar Imposto de Renda em 2026 — Agência Brasil

> Fetched 2026-09-05 · State news agency reporting the Receita's published rules. Secondary, but
> the figures are corroborated across every source read.

## What it says

- Taxable income: *"quem recebeu mais do que **R$ 35.584,00** em valores que precisam de ajuste de
  imposto"*.
- Assets: *"Possuíam, em 31/12/2025, bens ou direitos de valor total superior a **R$ 800.000,00**"*.
- The reference year is **ano-calendário 2025**, filed in 2026: *"ganhos, patrimônio e gastos no
  ano de 2025"*.

## What it does not say

Anything about **ano-calendário 2026**, which is the year this site's calculators compute and which
is filed in 2027. That threshold is not published yet, and this fetch is the evidence that the
figure a visitor needs for the *current* filing is R$ 35.584,00 rather than either number the site
carried.
