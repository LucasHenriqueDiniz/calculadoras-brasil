# Lei nº 9.250, de 26 de dezembro de 1995, art. 4º — Planalto

> Fetched 2026-10-18 in the browser pane. Read to settle which deductions the MONTHLY withholding base may take.

## What it says

"Art. 4º. Na determinação da base de cálculo sujeita à incidência mensal do imposto de renda
poderão ser deduzidas:" followed by these items, in their current wording:

- I — the amounts of art. 6º of Lei 8.134/1990 (livro-caixa);
- II — court-ordered alimony;
- III — a fixed amount per dependant;
- IV — contributions to official social security (INSS);
- V — contributions to domestic private pension plans paid by the taxpayer;
- VI — the exempt part of retirement income from age 65.

## What it does not say

- **Health and education are not on the list.** They are deductions of art. 8º, the annual
  adjustment. A monthly net-salary calculator that subtracts them from the withholding base is
  applying an annual rule to a monthly question.
