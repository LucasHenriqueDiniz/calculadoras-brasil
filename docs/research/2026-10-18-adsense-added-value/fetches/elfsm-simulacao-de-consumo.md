# Empresa Luz e Força Santa Maria — Simulação de consumo

> Fetched 2026-10-18 in the browser pane: position 1 for "calculadora conta de luz por aparelho". The page of a distributor in Espírito Santo. Read for its form, its formula and its reference figures.

## What it says

- **Form:** three fields, "Potência do aparelho (Watts)", "Horas de uso por dia" and "Dias de uso
  durante o mês". One appliance at a time. The result is "Gasto estimado" in kWh only, with no
  tariff and no reais.
- **Formula:** consumption in kWh = power (W) × hours of use per day × days of use in the month,
  divided by 1.000. The same formula as ours.
- **Reference list:** 25 appliances with a power each, among them "Geladeira: 130W",
  "Freezer: 130W", "Chuveiro: 3500W", "Máquina de lavar: 350W" and "Microondas: 1200W". No
  typical hours of use and no source for the figures.
- **Length:** about 292 words of body text, counted in the browser pane.

## What it does not say

- That a refrigerator's compressor cycles. Read with its own formula, "Geladeira: 130W" for 24 h
  and 30 days is 93,6 kWh a month.
- What any of it costs. No tariff appears on the page.
