# Simulador de alíquotas efetivas — Receita Federal

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · Position 1 on Google Brasil for "calculadora irpf 2026" (2: contabeis.com.br; 3: brasilprev; 4: dieese; 5: ecalculos; 6: contcontec; 7: unifebe; 8: fatorialinvest; 9: gov.br's 2026 tables).

## What it says

- 132 words on the page. Two tabs, "Cálculo Mensal" and "Cálculo Anual".
- **Monthly tab:** year 2026, month, taxable income, and one "Deduções" field pre-filled with
  607,20, then base, tax and effective rate.
- **Annual tab:** "Exercício 2026", taxable income, one "Deduções" field, base, tax, "Deduções
  especiais", tax due and effective rate.

## What it does not say

- It never compares the complete and simplified returns, never breaks deductions down into
  dependants, health, education or pension, and explains none of its figures.
- **Its annual tab is "Exercício 2026"**: the return filed in 2026, on 2025 income. This site's
  IRPF calculator models **ano-calendário 2026**, filed in 2027. The query "IRPF 2026" covers
  both, which the page has to say up front.
