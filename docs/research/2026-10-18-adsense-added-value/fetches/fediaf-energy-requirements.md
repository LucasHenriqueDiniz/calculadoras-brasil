# FEDIAF Nutritional Guidelines — energy requirements of adult dogs and cats

> Fetched 2026-10-18 with WebFetch and converted with `pdftotext -layout`: the September 2025 edition from europeanpetfood.org (the "current" one on that page, SHA-256 `0650ad06…`), and the July 2024 edition hosted by UK Pet Food (`cb8e6de9…`). Read for how much a pet eats, the largest item in the custo-pet calculator.

## What it says

- **Table VII-6**, "Practical recommendations for MER in dogs at different ages", kcal ME per
  kg BW^0,75: 1–2 years 130 (125–140); 3–7 years 110 (95–130); > 7 years (senior dogs) 95
  (80–120). The same in both editions.
- **§ 7.2.4.2, cats:** "FEDIAF recommendations for normal active adult cats are in accordance
  with NRC ... 100 kcal/kg BW0.67". "For indoor and/or neutered adult cats the average
  maintenance energy requirement is estimated to be 75 kcal/kg BW0.67". Table VII-9 gives 52–75
  for neutered or indoor cats and 100 for active cats. The same in both editions.
- **The values are starting points:** Table VII-6 "only starting points, the amount of energy a
  particular dog will finally need is significantly influenced by other factors such as
  activity, environment, breed, temperament ... body condition or disease".
- **Table VII-7**, by activity: 95 for low activity, 110 and 125 for moderate, and 150–175 for
  high.

## What it does not say

- The energy of any Brazilian dry food. The calculator takes it from the visitor's bag, and the
  page's table uses an example of 3.500 kcal/kg, stated as an example.
- Anything about Brazil. FEDIAF is the European pet food industry's federation; its energy
  figures follow the NRC and published studies, which it cites.
