# IDEC — Eletrobrás's monthly consumption for refrigerators and freezers

> Fetched 2026-10-18 in the browser pane. An IDEC press release dated 15/02/2013, "Atualizado" on the same date. Its "Na cozinha" section gives each figure with "Fonte: Eletrobrás". Read for what a refrigerator consumes in a month, against the 130 W × 24 h the calculator used.

## What it says

| appliance | per month | note on the page |
|---|---|---|
| Geladeira 1 porta | 25,2 kWh | "consumo ininterrupto" |
| Geladeira 1 porta frost-free | 39,6 kWh | "consumo ininterrupto" |
| Geladeira 2 portas | 48,24 kWh | "consumo ininterrupto" |
| Geladeira 2 portas frost-free | 56,88 kWh | "consumo ininterrupto" |
| Freezer | 47,55 kWh | "consumo ininterrupto" |

Spread over 24 h × 30 days, 720 hours, these are average draws of 35, 55, 67, 79 and 66 W.

## What it does not say

- The year, model or efficiency class the figures were measured on. They are 2013 averages; a new
  Procel A appliance may consume less.
- Anything about showers or air conditioners. The page does not list them.
- The Eletrobrás document itself. This is IDEC quoting it, not the primary table, which was not
  found.
