# Rio Claro — Calculadora custo do carro

> Fetched 2026-10-18 in the browser pane: position 1 for "calculadora custo de carro". An investment adviser's calculator. Read for its inputs, its result and what it leaves out.

## What it says

- **Inputs:** car value, propulsion type (combustion, hybrid or electric), km per day, one km/l, one
  fuel price, insurance a year, IPVA as a percentage of the value, maintenance level,
  first-year depreciation, an opportunity cost a year, parking and care, and financing (down
  payment, term, CET). It also asks Uber and public-transport fares, for a comparison.
- **Result, at its defaults:** "R$ 3.918 por mês", of which depreciation R$ 1.250 and "Custo do
  capital" R$ 1.167, the money tied up in the car at 14% a year. Then a comparison with Uber
  and public transport, and a five-year table with depreciation that falls each year.
- **Length:** about 573 words, counted in the browser pane. It asks for contact details after
  three simulations.

## What it does not say

- Anything about ethanol. It takes one fuel price and one km/l, so it cannot compare the two
  fuels of a flex car.
- Fuel prices: the visitor types one, with no state average.
