# Add a Byline Date to Google Search Results — Google Search Central

> Fetched 2026-11-14 (footer: "Last updated 2025-12-10 UTC") · Read to see what Google documents about `dateModified` and visible dates.

## What it says

- "A *byline date* is the date that Google estimates that the web page was updated or published."
- "Google doesn't depend on a single date factor because all factors can be prone to issues."
- "That's why our systems look at several factors to determine our best estimate of when a page was published or significantly updated."
- Recommends you "specify the `datePublished` and/or `dateModified` fields" on a CreativeWork subtype such as Article or BlogPosting.
- "Add a user-visible date to the page and feature it prominently."
- "Label your dates appropriately with text like "Publish" or "Last updated"."

## What it does not say

- It does not say dates, `dateModified` or freshness affect ranking. It only says Google may show the date in results.
- It does not cover non-article pages (a data page is not an Article/BlogPosting) and does not warn here about date manipulation — that warning is in `google-creating-helpful-content.md`.
