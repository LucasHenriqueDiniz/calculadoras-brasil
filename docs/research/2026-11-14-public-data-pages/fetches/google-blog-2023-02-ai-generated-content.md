# Google Search's guidance about AI-generated content — Google Search Central Blog

> Fetched 2026-11-14 · Post dated "Wednesday, February 8, 2023". Read for Google's own examples of acceptable automated content, which are data-driven pages.

## What it says

- "Automation has long been used to generate helpful content, such as sports scores, weather forecasts, and transcripts."
- "This said, it's important to recognize that not all use of automation, including AI generation, is spam."
- "Appropriate use of AI or automation is not against our guidelines."
- The violation is "Using automation—including AI—to generate content with the primary purpose of manipulating ranking in search results".

## What it does not say

- It names sports scores and weather forecasts (structured, frequently updated data) as helpful automation, which is the closest Google comes to blessing data-driven pages — but it gives no criteria for how much per-page value such pages need, and does not mention price or tariff data.
