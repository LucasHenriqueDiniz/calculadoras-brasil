# A guide to Google Search ranking systems — Google Search Central

> Fetched 2026-11-14 (footer: "Last updated 2025-12-10 UTC") · Read for the only primary statement on freshness, and for "original content".

## What it says

- Freshness: "We have various "query deserves freshness" systems designed to show fresher content for queries where it would be expected."
- Its examples are a just-released movie and a recent earthquake ("if an earthquake happened recently, then news articles and fresher content might appear").
- Original content: "We have systems to help ensure we are showing original content prominently in search results," "including original reporting," ahead of those who merely cite it.
- Helpful content: "In March 2024, it evolved and became part of" "our core ranking systems".
- "We employ a range of spam detection systems, including SpamBrain," "to deal with content and behaviors that violate our spam policies."

## What it does not say

- It does not say whether price queries ("preço gasolina hoje") are treated as freshness-seeking, nor which signal (visible date, `dateModified`, crawl) the freshness systems read.
- The "original content" system is about original reporting vs citing it; it does not say how a page that re-publishes a government dataset with its own analysis ranks against the government source.
