# Ranking das Tarifas (ANEEL)

> Fetched 2026-11-14 · ANEEL's explanatory page for its tariff ranking. Read to see what ANEEL offers and how it qualifies the numbers.

## What it says

- ANEEL publishes the ranking of current approved tariffs in R$/kWh.
- The values exclude ICMS, PIS/Pasep, Cofins, the public-lighting fee and the bandeira surcharge.
- The ranking includes the conventional tariff and "horária branca" by "postos tarifários".
- Points to the interactive report under "Centrais de Conteúdos > Relatórios e Indicadores > Tarifas e Informações Econômico-Financeiras" and an Excel download.
- "Publicado em 25/02/2022 09:32", "Modificado em 04/08/2023 15:55".

## What it does not say

- No history, no update cadence, no reference date of the data on this page.
