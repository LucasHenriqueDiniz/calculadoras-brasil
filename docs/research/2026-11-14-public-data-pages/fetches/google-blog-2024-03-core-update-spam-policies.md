# What web creators should know about our March 2024 core update and new spam policies — Google Search Central Blog

> Fetched 2026-11-14 · Post dated "Tuesday, March 5, 2024". Read because this is where scaled content abuse replaced the older "spammy automatically-generated content" policy.

## What it says

- Scaled content abuse "is when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating Search rankings and not helping users."
- "This new policy builds on our previous spam policy about automatically-generated content,"
- "ensuring that we can take action on scaled content abuse as needed, no matter whether content is produced"
- "through automation, human efforts, or some combination of human and automated processes."
- FAQ: the policy is "meant to help people focus more clearly on the idea that producing content at scale is abusive if done for the purpose of manipulating search rankings" "and that this applies whether automation or humans are involved."
- The helpful content system was folded into core ranking in this update (confirmed separately in `google-ranking-systems-guide.md`).

## What it does not say

- No example in the scaled-content section; no mention of data-driven, programmatic, location or dataset pages.
- Nothing that distinguishes "many pages from a real dataset" from "many pages from a keyword list". The line is drawn only by purpose and value.
