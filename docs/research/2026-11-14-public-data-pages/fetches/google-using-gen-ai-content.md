# Google Search's guidance on using generative AI content on your website — Google Search Central

> Fetched 2026-11-14 (footer: "Last updated 2026-10-01 UTC") · Read in case it says anything about automatically generated, data-driven pages at scale.

## What it says

- "using generative AI tools or other similar tools to generate many pages without adding value for users may violate" "Google's spam policy on scaled content abuse".
- It cites the rater guidelines' lowest rating for "main content created with little to no effort, little to no originality, and little to no added value (section 4.6.6)."
- "When creating content for the web, focus on accuracy, quality, and relevance," "especially when automatically generating the content."
- "If you're automatically generating content, consider adding information on how your content was created".
- The review requirement "also applies to metadata like" "title elements, meta description elements, structured data, and alternate texts for images".

## What it does not say

- Nothing specific to pages built from structured/open data rather than generative text. The "how it was created" disclosure is the only concrete recommendation that transfers (e.g. "dados da ANP, levantamento semanal, atualizado em …").
