# John Mueller on Bluesky: programmatic SEO thread (2026-09-07)

> Fetched 2026-11-14 via the public AppView API (`public.api.bsky.app/xrpc/app.bsky.feed.getPostThread`, no login), because the bsky.app page renders client-side. A Googler's personal reply, not documentation.

## What it says

- Context: a site owner (2026-09-06) described generated pages and said he even built a Google-only sitemap listing only pages "that have more than 10 detections of tech so pages are not thin".
- Mueller (2026-09-07T12:13Z), five posts. Short quotes:
  - It "sounds like a programmatic SEO play" iterating "domain names, technologies, and attributes".
  - "You end up with tons of pages that have "some" value".
  - Programmatic SEO "like this often leads to a site that's either spam, borderline spam, or low quality".
  - Spinning up many pages is easy; "it's hard to provide real value to users".
  - Google's systems "have possibly lost faith in your site" because of "the old pages".
  - "Resolving this tends to take time & significant effort to show the value."
- He frames it as site-level, not page-level, and adds a caveat: "not to say that your site is spam".

## What it does not say

- No threshold or test for when data-driven pages are fine. "Like this" scopes the remark to combinatorial pages (every combination of lists), not to pages each backed by a distinct dataset row with history.
- Does not say that filtering thin pages out of a sitemap helps — the owner had already done that and Mueller did not endorse it.
