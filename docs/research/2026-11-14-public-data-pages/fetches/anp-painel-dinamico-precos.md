## What it says

- Shows average prices, weekly and/or monthly, by product and "abrangência geográfica", for resellers, distributors and producers/importers.
- Embedded Power BI report (`app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiMGM0NDhhMTUt...`).
- Page published 10/12/2020, updated 29/10/2026.
- Data basis: Levantamento de Preços (Resolução ANP nº 729/2018, Portaria ANP nº 297/2001).
