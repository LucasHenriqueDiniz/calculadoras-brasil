# Série histórica do levantamento de preços (ANP)

> Fetched 2026-11-14 · Read to find the weekly historical series by state and by municipality that a per-state history page would be built on.

## What it says

- File base: `https://www.gov.br/anp/pt-br/assuntos/precos-e-defesa-da-concorrencia/precos/precos-revenda-e-de-distribuicao-combustiveis/shlp/`
- "Série histórica semanal" — "Maio de 2004 a dezembro de 2012": Brasil, Regiões, Estados (`.xlsx`), Municípios (`.xlsb`).
- "Série histórica semanal" — "A partir de 2013" ("Atualizado em 13/11/2026"): `semanal/semanal-estados-desde-2013.xlsx`, `semanal-brasil-desde-2013.xlsx`, `semanal-regioes-desde-2013.xlsx`; municipalities split into files 2013–2014 (`.xlsb`), 2015–2017 (`.xlsb`), 2018–2021 (`.xls`), 2022–2023, 2024–2025, 2026 (`.xlsx`).
- "Série histórica mensal" — "Julho de 2001 a dezembro de 2012" and "A partir de 2013" ("Atualizado em 10/11/2026"), same geographic levels.
- Fuels: gasolina C, etanol hidratado, óleo diesel B, GNV, GLP P13.
- Methodology note: "Até 30 de outubro de 2004, todos os preços médios eram calculados por média aritmética simples." After that, state/regional/national averages are weighted by distributor sales; municipal averages stay simple means.
- Survey history: 411 municipalities 2001 → May 2004, then 555 localities from 2 May 2004.

## What it does not say

- No licence or terms of reuse on this page (the per-station series on dados.gov.br carries "Creative Commons Attribution" — see `dados-gov-br-serie-historica-precos-combustiveis-glp.md`; that label is not stated for these aggregate xlsx files).
- No formal publication schedule; only "Atualizado em" dates. No CSV of the state series — xlsx only.
