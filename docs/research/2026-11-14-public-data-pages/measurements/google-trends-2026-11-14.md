# Google Trends, Brazil, last 12 months (2025-11-09 .. 2026-11-08, weekly)

Read from trends.google.com in the owner's Chrome, "Pesquisa na Web", "Todas as categorias". The values
are Trends' own index: 100 is the peak week of the most-searched term **in that comparison**, and every
other number is relative to it. They are not search counts.

**What these numbers are.** MEASURED relative interest. Comparisons share a term so they can be chained:
`valor do kwh` sits in all three and reads 3 in each, and `calculadora salário líquido` reads 1 in both
comparisons where it appears.

## What this does not give

- **No absolute volume.** Trends never returns counts. Turning the index into searches per month needs
  one term with a known count, and no such anchor was available in this session (see the research's
  volume section for what was tried).
- Terms that read 0 or 1 are below Trends' resolution here, so "bandeira tarifária = 0" means "much
  smaller than preço gasolina", not "nobody searches it".
