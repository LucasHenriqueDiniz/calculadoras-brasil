# Como comparar salário CLT e renda como PJ — Agilize

> Fetched 2026-09-05 · Read for the worked equivalence case, which is the closest thing to an
> oracle found for the figure `/calculadora-clt-vs-pj` publishes.

## What it says

- Simples Nacional, **Anexo III** (Fator R ≥ 28%): *"alíquotas menores entre 6% e 19,5%"*.
- **Anexo V** (Fator R < 28%): 6% to 33% by revenue band.
- Fator R is *"a proporção entre folha de pagamento e receita bruta dos últimos 12 meses"*,
  threshold **28%**.
- A PJ's net is the invoice less the DAS, less accounting, less *"INSS e IR sobre o pró-labore"* —
  note: on the **pró-labore**, not on the invoice.
- **Worked case:** a CLT salary of R$ 10.000 (net ≈ R$ 8.200) is matched by invoicing
  **R$ 13.000** (Anexo III) to **R$ 14.000** (Anexo V). Rule of thumb *"aumentos de 30% a 50%"*.

## What it does not say

The band composition of the DAS, and nothing that would let the figure be recomputed from first
principles rather than quoted.
