# Lei Complementar nº 123/2006 — art. 18 and Anexos III and V — Planalto

> Fetched 2026-10-18 in the browser pane. Statute text, free to reproduce (Lei 9.610/1998, art. 8º, IV). Planalto prints superseded wordings beside the current ones; only the current wording, as indicated by its "Redação dada pela LC 155/2016" marker, is kept here.

## What it says

**Art. 18, caput (LC 155/2016):** the monthly amount is found by applying the *alíquotas efetivas*,
computed from the nominal rates of Anexos I to V, to the base of § 3º.

**§ 1º:** the nominal rate is chosen by the gross revenue of the previous twelve months (RBT12).

**§ 1º-A:** "A alíquota efetiva é o resultado de: (RBT12 × Aliq − PD) / RBT12", where RBT12 is that
revenue, Aliq the nominal rate and PD the "parcela a deduzir" of the annex.

**§ 5º-D, IV and VI:** taxed under **Anexo III**: "elaboração de programas de computadores, inclusive
jogos eletrônicos, desde que desenvolvidos em estabelecimento do optante", and "planejamento,
confecção, manutenção e atualização de páginas eletrônicas", on the same condition.

**§ 5º-I:** taxed under **Anexo V**, among others: VI "engenharia, … testes, suporte e análises técnicas
e tecnológicas, pesquisa, design, desenho"; IX "auditoria, economia, consultoria, gestão,
organização, controle e administração"; XII other intellectual services not in Anexos III or IV.

**§ 5º-J:** the § 5º-I activities move to **Anexo III** "caso a razão entre a folha de salários e a
receita bruta da pessoa jurídica seja igual ou superior a 28%".

**§ 5º-M:** when that ratio is **below 28%**, the activities of § 5º-D (software, websites) move to
**Anexo V**.

20% social security contribution inside the DAS, not on the payroll.

## What it does not say

- **What "folha de salários" includes, as read.** § 24 of art. 18 defines it, and it was not
  extracted. The model treats the pró-labore as the whole payroll of a one-person company, with
  no patronal contribution outside the DAS. That is consistent with the annexes but not quoted.
- **2026 under the tax reform.** Both annexes carry "Vide Lei Complementar nº 214, de 2025", and
  LC 214/2025 was not read. The tables are the ones Planalto prints as in force; whether
  CBS/IBS changes the DAS in 2026 is **not established**.
