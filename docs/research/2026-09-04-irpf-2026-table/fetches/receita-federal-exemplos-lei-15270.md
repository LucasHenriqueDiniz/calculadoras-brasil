# Exemplos de Aplicação da Lei 15.270/2025 — Receita Federal

> Fetched 2026-09-04 · The tax authority's own worked examples. Read as the independent oracle
> for the formula quoted from the law: a formula and a worked number that agree are worth more
> than either alone.

## What it says

Second band, monthly:
"R$ 978,62 - (0,133145 x rendimentos tributáveis sujeitos à incidência mensal)"

Above R$ 7.350,00 no reduction is permitted (per § 2º of Lei nº 9.250, de 26 de dezembro de 1995).

**Worked example, quoted:** a monthly salary of R$ 6.000,00 with R$ 649,60 of deductions gives a
taxable base of R$ 5.350,40 and a calculated tax of R$ 562,63. The reduction is
`R$ 978,62 − (0,133145 × R$ 6.000,00) = R$ 179,75`, leaving R$ 382,88.

Note the example applies the coefficient to **gross monthly income (R$ 6.000,00)**, not to the
taxable base (R$ 5.350,40). That distinction is not obvious from the formula alone and it changes
the answer.

## What it does not say

No annual worked example. The annual formula in this research comes from the law text
(`camara-lei-15270-2025.md`) and was verified by independent arithmetic instead — see
`../research.md`, Findings §4.
