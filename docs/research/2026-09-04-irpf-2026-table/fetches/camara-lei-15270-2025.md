# Lei nº 15.270, de 26 de novembro de 2025 — publicação original

> Fetched 2026-09-04 · The law itself, as published. Read to get the redutor formula and its
> limits from the text rather than from a summary of it.

## What it says

**Monthly reduction (Art. 3º-A):**

- Income up to R$ 5.000,00 — reduction of "até R$ 312,89 (de modo que o imposto devido seja zero)"
- Income R$ 5.000,01 to R$ 7.350,00 —
  "R$ 978,62 - (0,133145 x rendimentos tributáveis sujeitos à incidência mensal)"
- The reduction phases out linearly to zero at R$ 7.350,00.

**Annual reduction (Art. 11-A):**

- Income up to R$ 60.000,00 — reduction of "até R$ 2.694,15 (de modo que o imposto devido seja zero)"
- Income R$ 60.000,01 to R$ 88.200,00 —
  "R$ 8.429,73 - (0,095575 x rendimentos tributáveis sujeitos ao ajuste anual)"
- The reduction phases out linearly to zero at R$ 88.200,00.

Both provisions cap the reduction at the tax actually calculated, so the reduction can zero the
tax but never turn it into a refund.

## What it does not say

The law does not restate the progressive table. It reduces the tax that table produces, which is
why the two documents have to be read together.
